NiviCap Powers Students With Up To Rs 45 Lakh In Hassle Free Digital Loans

BW Education December 02, 2025

NiviCap offered education loans up to Rs 45 lakh that cover tuition, living expenses, visa costs and travel and competitive interest rates based on creditworthiness.

Backed by the founders of Ziksa, Australia’s homegrown fintech, it combines knowledge of the Indian market with on-the-ground experience in Australia so that parents know precisely where their money is going and when. The NiviCap App makes it easier for them to send and receive money, pick up students at the airport and support them when they arrive, so they do not have to navigate a new country on their own. Everything from applying to receiving funds to living in Australia is designed to be quick, straightforward and digital, with human support at moments when it is needed most.

This ensures that students can focus on their future, while parents experience reduced stress at every stage. Strengthening this bridge of trust is the presence of Justin Langer, former Australian cricket coach, who supports NiviCap as its brand ambassador, reflecting the platform’s values of guidance, reliability and mentorship.

Karthik Srinivasan, Founder, NiviCap, said, “As an Indian-born Australian, I have lived the same journey that lakhs of Indian students aspire to every year. Today, around 5.2 lakh Indian students want to study in Australia, but only about 1.6 lakh make it, often because the financial process feels overwhelming, fragmented or unclear. Nearly 80 per cent depend on loans, yet many families struggle with documentation, cross-border regulations and the emotional stress of managing finances across two countries. Having worked in banking across India and Australia, I have seen firsthand how these challenges can make academic dreams feel distant.”
