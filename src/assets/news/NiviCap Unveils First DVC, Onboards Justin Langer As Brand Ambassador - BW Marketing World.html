NiviCap Unveils First DVC, Onboards Justin Langer As Brand Ambassador

BW Online Bureau November 21, 2025

NiviCap has unveiled its first brand digital video commercial (DVC) nationwide. The campaign features former Australian cricket coach and global cricketing figure Justin Langer, who also joins the company as its official Brand Ambassador.

The DVC has gone live across digital platforms and marks a key marketing push for the company as it focuses on technology-led transparency and family-focused support in the study-abroad financing segment. Positioned as a one-stop digital platform, NiviCap integrates education loan discovery, financial planning, forex services, documentation, and student support for those heading to Australia.

Rooted in the brand philosophy ‘Fast. Fair. Family Approved.’ The campaign film depicts the emotional and financial challenges associated with overseas education. It highlights how the platform aims to offer structured guidance to both students and parents through its consolidated, tech-driven services. The film uses a cross-cultural narrative and presents Justin Langer as a mentor-like figure offering reassurance during critical life transitions.

India has become the second-largest source country for international students in Australia, with about 137,703 Indian students commencing or continuing studies in 2025. Despite the growing numbers, education financing and related processes continue to remain scattered. NiviCap aims to address this gap, especially for families in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities, by positioning itself as a unified and transparent digital platform.

Commenting on the launch, Karthik Srinivasan, Founder, NiviCap, said, “Today marks a proud moment for us as our first-ever brand DVC goes live nationwide. Onboarding Justin Langer as our Brand Ambassador is a reflection of the values we stand for, trust, discipline, mentorship, and genuine care for young achievers. Our campaign captures the core emotion behind NiviCap: every student deserves a smooth, transparent, and supportive path to their global dreams. Justin embodies that spirit perfectly, and through this DVC we aim to reassure families that they are not alone in navigating the study-abroad journey”.

Expressing his views, Justin Langer, Brand Ambassador and former Australian cricket coach, said, “As both a father and a coach, I understand the emotions that come with watching young people chase big dreams away from home. India and Australia share a bond that goes beyond sport, it’s built on trust, respect, and shared ambition. NiviCap captures that beautifully. To me, NiviCap is like a great coach, steady, reliable, and always there when the pressure is on. It gives students the confidence to perform and parents the reassurance that their children are in safe hands. That’s why I’m proud to stand with a brand that champions both ambition and care.”

Mukund Narayanamurti, Minister (Commercial) and Head of South Asia, Australian Trade and Investment Commission (Austrade), added, “Australia deeply values its education partnership with India. Indian students enrich our campuses and communities, and they are central to the cultural and economic ties between our two nations. NiviCap’s launch by an Australian firm Ziksu, is a great initiative to support Indian students aspiring to Study in Australia.”

India’s growing presence in Australia’s international student community has led to increased demand for organised financial and documentation support. The campaign introduces NiviCap’s services within this context, focusing on the processes students and families navigate during overseas education planning.
