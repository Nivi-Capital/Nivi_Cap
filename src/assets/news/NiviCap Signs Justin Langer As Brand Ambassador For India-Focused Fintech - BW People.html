NiviCap Signs Justin Langer As Brand Ambassador For India-Focused Fintech

BW People November 20, 2025

NiviCap has announced Australian cricket legend Justin Langer as its Brand Ambassador, unveiling its first nationwide digital video campaign aimed at students planning to study in Australia. The platform, positioned as India’s first dedicated fintech solution for the Australia study-abroad ecosystem, is rolling out the film across digital channels starting today.


The campaign marks a defining brand milestone for NiviCap as it strengthens its positioning around transparent financing, family-oriented support and a unified digital experience. The platform integrates education loan discovery, documentation, forex assistance, financial planning and on-ground support, targeting students and families seeking a simpler and more reliable pathway to overseas education.


Built around the philosophy “Fast. Fair. Family Approved.”, the film captures the emotional realities of financing an international education while highlighting how NiviCap streamlines the journey for both students and parents. Langer appears as a mentor and guide in the narrative, reinforcing the message of trust, reassurance and steady support during critical transitions.


With India now the second-largest source of international students in Australia, demand for structured financing solutions continues to rise, particularly across Tier-II and Tier-III cities where families often navigate fragmented processes. NiviCap aims to bridge this gap through a transparent, tech-backed system designed to remove complexity from the study-abroad experience.


Founder Karthik Srinivasan said the campaign represents a proud milestone for the company, adding that Langer’s presence reflects values of mentorship, trust and discipline that mirror NiviCap’s mission. Langer, expressing enthusiasm for the partnership, likened the platform to a dependable coach who offers clarity under pressure, supporting both students’ ambitions and parents’ confidence.


The Australian Trade and Investment Commission also welcomed the initiative, citing the deep and growing education partnership between India and Australia. With its first campaign now live, NiviCap is positioning itself as a trusted digital companion for every Indian student preparing to pursue higher education in Australia.
