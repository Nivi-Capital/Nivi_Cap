NiviCap Onboards Justin Langer as Brand Ambassador for India’s First Fintech Platform for Students Aspiring to Study in Australia

Passionate in Marketing November 21, 2025

National | November 20, 2025: NiviCap, India’s first-of-its-kind fintech digital solutions platform for students aspiring to study in Australia, today announced the nationwide launch of its debut brand DVC featuring global cricketing icon Justin Langer, who also joins the brand as its official Brand Ambassador.

Going live across digital platforms starting today, the DVC marks a major brand moment for NiviCap as it sharpens its marketing narrative around technology-led transparency and family-first support in the study-abroad financing ecosystem. Positioned as a fintech, one-stop digital platform, NiviCap brings together education loan discovery, financial planning, forex, documentation, and on-ground student support under one unified experience.

The campaign film is rooted in NiviCap’s core philosophy, “Fast. Fair. Family Approved.” It brings alive the emotional and financial complexities of the overseas education journey while showcasing how NiviCap acts as a trustworthy, tech-enabled partner for both students and parents. Through a warm, cross-cultural storyline, the DVC highlights how the platform simplifies loan discovery, documentation, financial planning, and on-ground support for students headed to Australia.

Featuring Justin Langer as the voice of reassurance, the film positions him as a mentor figure, someone who understands ambition, pressure, and the importance of having reliable guidance during life’s most defining transitions.

India is now the second-largest source country for international students in Australia, with approximately 137,703 students from India commencing or continuing study in 2025. Despite such strong demand, the process of education financing and associated requirements remains fragmented. NiviCap aims to simplify this journey with a unified, trustworthy, and transparent fintech-led platform, empowering families, especially from Tier-II and Tier-III cities, to pursue their overseas dreams with clarity and confidence.

Mr. Karthik Srinivasan, Founder, NiviCap, shared, “Today marks a proud moment for us as our first-ever brand DVC goes live nationwide. Onboarding Justin Langer as our Brand Ambassador is a reflection of the values we stand for, trust, discipline, mentorship, and genuine care for young achievers. Our campaign captures the core emotion behind NiviCap: every student deserves a smooth, transparent, and supportive path to their global dreams. Justin embodies that spirit perfectly, and through this DVC we aim to reassure families that they are not alone in navigating the study-abroad journey”.

Expressing his enthusiasm, Mr. Justin Langer, Brand Ambassador and former Australian cricket coach, said, “As both a father and a coach, I understand the emotions that come with watching young people chase big dreams away from home. India and Australia share a bond that goes beyond sport, it’s built on trust, respect, and shared ambition. NiviCap captures that beautifully. To me, NiviCap is like a great coach, steady, reliable, and always there when the pressure is on. It gives students the confidence to perform and parents the reassurance that their children are in safe hands. That’s why I’m proud to stand with a brand that champions both ambition and care.”

Mr Mukund Narayanamurti, Minister (Commercial), and Head of South Asia, Australian Trade and Investment Commission (Austrade), said, “Australia deeply values its education partnership with India. Indian students enrich our campuses and communities, and they are central to the cultural and economic ties between our two nations. NiviCap’s launch by an Australian firm Ziksu is a great initiative to support Indian students aspiring to Study in Australia.”

With India emerging as the second-largest source of international students in Australia, the brand’s campaign aims to establish NiviCap as the country’s most trusted digital partner for education loans, forex, guidance, and post-arrival support.

Positioned as warm, trustworthy, and family-aligned, NiviCap’s first DVC sets the tone for its brand identity: Study in Australia — no limits, just your dream.
